approve (anonymous, real STARK): inner msig guest 1,048,576 total cycles
  (673,043-687,920 user, 1 segment) ~4.5-4.6 min; outer privacy circuit
  4,718,592-5,242,880 total cycles (5 segments) ~26-28 min; succinct receipt
  ~261.5 KB. Wall ~30 min per approve on the host above.
The outer circuit grows with the proposal's nullifier set (4,718,592 for the
first approve, 5,242,880 for the second). The outer prover needs >6 GB RAM: a
3.8 GB host OOM-killed r0vm on the identical transaction.

plain public transactions: sub-second RISC-V execution, no STARK.
